PNW Transit - changelog
=======================

v1 (2026-10-31)
- New: BC Transit Enviro500 double-decker, in the red, white and blue livery
  and (as a variant) the 2024 green livery.
- New: TransLink New Flyer XDE60 articulated bus.
- New: Seattle Streetcar Inekon 12 Trio and Portland Streetcar Skoda 10 T.
- New: Sound Transit Link Kinkisharyo and Siemens S70 LRVs, as trams.
- New: TransLink SkyTrain Mark V, a five-car EMU for electrified rail.
- New: Amtrak Cascades Airo: ALC-42E locomotive, coach and cab car.
